The Book

Where the numbers come from

Tides — LINZ official predictions for Westport, converted to Haast River Entrance using LINZ's own published secondary-port correction (port 6519: high and low water +30 min, range ratio 0.70 about mean sea level). Times are local wall clock with daylight saving already applied, exactly as LINZ publishes them. Two years are stored in this page, so tides and moon work with no signal at all.

Rain, cloud, wind — Open-Meteo forecast API. River flow — Open-Meteo flood API (Copernicus GloFAS discharge for the Waita catchment), read as a ratio against the median of its own last 60 observed days rather than as an absolute level, so it measures “up on normal for this river” instead of a number nobody can eyeball. Swell and sea — Open-Meteo marine API. Moon — computed in this page.

“Out there” is not part of the score. The Weather factor rates a dull, damp day highly because that is what suits the bait. Whether it is pleasant to stand in for three hours is the opposite question, so it is reported separately — conditions, real and felt temperature, rain and wind through the fishing window — and never folded into the number.

How the score is built

Each fishable high tide gets its own score; the day takes the best one. A window is only counted if it falls inside legal whitebaiting hours. Penalties for flood, drought and a dangerous mouth are subtracted after the weighted total, so one genuinely bad factor can sink an otherwise tidy-looking day.

Two ranges, on purpose. The full Index runs 16 days, which is as far as the rain, river and swell forecasts actually reach. Past that, The long game scores tide and moon only — both are arithmetic and known exactly for years ahead — keeping them in the same 3:1 proportion they hold in the real Index. It is named and scored separately because calling a five-week-out number a Lachie Index would be claiming knowledge nobody has. It answers one question: which days have the water behind them.

Whitebait season: 1 September – 30 October. Fishing hours 5am–8pm NZST, 6am–9pm NZDT once daylight saving starts. Check DOC for the current rules — they change, and this page does not.
